## 2. COUPLED PHYSICS ENGINE (`src/simulation/physicsEngine.ts`)

Every number, chart, 3D shader, and bounding box in the UI must be driven by a live 10 Hz coupled differential equation loop—never hardcoded static strings:

### A. 3D Crater Siting, Directional $B_4C$ Shadow Shield & Raycast Occlusion
- Allow the operator to drag/move the reactor position `(rx, rz)` inside the crater AND rotate its Boron-Carbide ($B_4C$) Directional Shadow Shield azimuth angle $\theta_{\text{shield}}$ (plus provide an `"Auto-Optimize Siting"` button).
- Cast rays using `THREE.Raycaster` from the reactor core toward 36 azimuthal sectors and directly toward the Habitat Dome position.
- Compute radiation dose at distance $d$ with regolith intersection thickness $x$ (meters of crater terrain intersected by the raycaster) and directional shadow-plug attenuation $S(\Delta\theta)$ ($0.04$ inside the $\pm 25^\circ$ $B_4C$ shadow shield cone, $1.0$ outside):
  $$I(d, x, \Delta\theta) = \frac{I_0 \cdot S(\Delta\theta)}{d^2} \cdot e^{-\mu_{\text{regolith}} \cdot x}$$
- When the Habitat vector is protected by the shadow shield and/or terrain occlusion ($I < 5.0\text{ rem/yr}$), render the Habitat cone in **Green** (`HABITAT DOME SECURE`). Unshielded lateral sectors hitting the crater wall render as **Red Hazard Cones** (`HAZARD SHADOW`). If the user drags the reactor into an unshielded line-of-sight alignment, the Habitat cone dynamically turns Red and triggers a radiation warning in the diagnostic log.

### B. Vacuum Stefan-Boltzmann Heat Rejection & Lunar Dust Thermal Choking
- Reactor thermal power $P_{\text{th}} = 175\text{ kWt}$ nominal. Lunar night sink temperature $T_{\text{sink}} = 40\text{ K}$.
- Each of the 4 radiator wings ($i \in \{0,1,2,3\}$, surface area $A_i = 42\text{ m}^2$) has a dust fouling fraction $D_i \in [0, 1]$.
- Effective radiator emissivity drops linearly with silicate dust coverage:
  $$\epsilon_i(D_i) = 0.90 - 0.54 \cdot D_i$$
  (At $D_0 = 0.89$ dust fouling on Wing 0, effective emissivity equals $0.42$, matching `RADIATOR EMISSIVITY: 0.42`).
- Heat rejected into hard vacuum per wing via Stefan-Boltzmann law:
  $$P_{\text{rad}, i} = \epsilon_i(D_i) \cdot \sigma \cdot A_i \cdot (T_{\text{wing}, i}^4 - T_{\text{sink}}^4)$$
- Why the fouled wing glows brighter: When dust insulates Wing 0 ($\epsilon_0 \rightarrow 0.42$), heat cannot escape efficiently, causing the internal NaK liquid-metal coolant manifold temperature $T_{\text{wing}, 0}$ and reactor core temperature $T_{\text{core}}$ to climb rapidly toward $920\text{ K}$ (`CORE TEMP: 920K`).
